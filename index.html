Institutional Excellence

Empowering Tribal & Rural Learners with 21st-Century Pedagogical Skills

Bridging Tradition, Academic Discipline & Professional Competence

Established in Peeth (Tehsil Simalwara, District Dungarpur), Gurukripa Shikshan Sansthan College stands as a beacon of enlightened education in the Vagad region. Our dual focus combines rigorous university degree programs (B.A., B.Sc.) with NCTE-recognized teacher preparation programs (B.Ed., Integrated 4-Year B.A. B.Ed. & B.Sc. B.Ed.).

"Our mission is to ensure every aspiring teacher and graduate from the tribal heartland acquires intellectual independence, professional mastery, and deep moral integrity."
