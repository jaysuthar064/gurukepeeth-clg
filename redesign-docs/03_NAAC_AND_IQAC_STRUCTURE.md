# Gurukripa Peeth Redesign — NAAC & IQAC Blueprint & Specifications
> Exhaustive structural, statutory, and visual specification for the **Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC)** and **NAAC Accreditation** portals, modeled after [GMGC Udaipur](https://gmgcudaipur.org/iqac/).

---

## 1. Statutory Context & Significance
For any recognized higher educational institution (HEI) affiliated with state universities (such as Govind Guru Tribal University, Banswara - GGTU) and regulated by UGC & NCTE, the **NAAC & IQAC web presence is a mandatory statutory requirement**. 

During NAAC assessment cycles, Peer Teams, DVV scrutiny, and stakeholders directly inspect these pages. The presentation must be:
- **Flawlessly organized into the 7 NAAC Criteria**
- **Transparent and easily accessible with one-click PDF downloads**
- **Equipped with active Stakeholder Feedback mechanisms and Action Taken Reports (ATRs)**
- **Authoritative, dignified, and visually impeccable**

- **Introduction**: History and mandate of IQAC at Gurukripa Peeth.
- **Primary Goals**:
  1. To develop a conscious, consistent, and catalytic action plan to improve academic and administrative performance.

### 3.2 The 7 Evaluative Criteria Framework (Core NAAC Pillar)

A clean interactive 7-card grid or multi-tab selector detailing the Seven Quality Criteria with direct links to supporting metrics and geo-tagged documentary evidence:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        THE 7 NAAC CRITERIA                             │
├───────────────────┬────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────┤
│ CRITERION 1       │ CRITERION 2        │ CRITERION 3                   │
│ Curricular        │ Teaching-Learning  │ Research, Innovations         │
│ Aspects           │ & Evaluation       │ & Extension                   │
├───────────────────┼────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────┤
│ CRITERION 4       │ CRITERION 5        │ CRITERION 6                   │
│ Infrastructure    │ Student Support    │ Governance, Leadership        │
│ & Learning Res.   │ & Progression      │ & Management                  │
├───────────────────┴────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────┤
│ CRITERION 7: Institutional Values & Best Practices                     │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### Detailed Breakdown of Criteria Pages:
1. **Criterion 1 — Curricular Aspects**:
   - Metric 1.1: Curriculum Design & Delivery
   - Metric 1.2: Academic Flexibility & Subject Combinations
   - Metric 1.3: Curriculum Enrichment & Value-Added Courses
   - Metric 1.4: Feedback System (Collection, Analysis & Action Taken)

2. **Criterion 2 — Teaching-Learning and Evaluation**:
   - Metric 2.1: Student Enrolment & Reservation Profiles
   - Metric 2.2: Catering to Student Diversity (Slow & Advanced Learners)
   - Metric 2.3: Teaching-Learning Process & ICT Tools
   - Metric 2.4: Teacher Profile & Quality (Ph.D., NET/SET)
   - Metric 2.5: Evaluation Process & Internal Assessment Grievances
   - Metric 2.6: Student Performance & Learning Outcomes (PO/PSO/CO)
   - Metric 2.7: Student Satisfaction Survey (SSS)

3. **Criterion 3 — Research, Innovations and Extension**:
   - Metric 3.1: Resource Mobilization for Research
   - Metric 3.2: Innovation Ecosystem
   - Metric 3.3: Research Publications & Books / Chapters
   - Metric 3.4: Extension Activities (NSS, Red Ribbon, Community Connect)
   - Metric 3.5: Collaboration, MOUs & Linkages

4. **Criterion 4 — Infrastructure and Learning Resources**:
   - Metric 4.1: Physical Facilities (Classrooms, Labs, Seminar Hall)
   - Metric 4.2: Library as a Learning Resource (ILMS, Books, Journals, NDL)
   - Metric 4.3: IT Infrastructure & Wi-Fi Bandwidth
   - Metric 4.4: Maintenance of Campus Infrastructure & Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

5. **Criterion 5 — Student Support and Progression**:
   - Metric 5.1: Student Support & Scholarships (Govt & Institutional)
   - Metric 5.2: Student Progression to Higher Education & Employment
   - Metric 5.3: Student Participation in Sports & Cultural Activities
   - Metric 5.4: Alumni Engagement & Contribution

6. **Criterion 6 — Governance, Leadership and Management**:
   - Metric 6.1: Institutional Vision & Leadership
   - Metric 6.2: Strategy Development & Deployment (Organogram)
   - Metric 6.3: Faculty Empowerment Strategies & Welfare Measures
   - Metric 6.4: Financial Management & Internal/External Audits
   - Metric 6.5: Internal Quality Assurance System (IQAC Initiatives)

7. **Criterion 7 — Institutional Values and Best Practices**:
   - Metric 7.1: Institutional Values & Social Responsibilities (Gender Equity, Solar, Waste Management, Divyangjan Accessibility)
   - Metric 7.2: Best Practices of the College
   - Metric 7.3: Institutional Distinctiveness
